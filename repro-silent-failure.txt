Where the error is swallowed:
- ContextImpl.ensureExternalDirsExistOrFilter() catches Exception, logs at W, sets dir = null.
- UserDataPreparer.prepareUserDataLI() catch: logs to uiderrors.txt, retries once, then continues boot
  (recovery wipe only for a *new* user 0 with volumeUuid == null).
- Nothing is surfaced to the user or to the app.
